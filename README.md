# HACA Budget versus Actual Expense Dashboard

**Overview**
A Power BI dashboard for monitoring departmental expenses against budget and reviewing monthly spending patterns.

**Business objective**
Give finance or operations teams a clear view of actual expenditure, budget allocation, variances, and monthly expense movement.

**Key KPIs and metrics**
Average monthly budget, average monthly expense, actual amount, budget amount, budget variance/difference, monthly actuals change, and budget status.

**Dashboard features**
Department, expense-category, and month filters; KPI cards; budget-status KPI; line, column, clustered-column, and donut charts.

**Insights and use cases**
Supports budget-control reviews, identification of overspending areas, departmental comparison, category analysis, and month-over-month expense monitoring.

**Tools and technologies**
Power BI; calculated measures; interactive slicers and KPI visuals.

**Skills demonstrated**
Budget analysis, variance analysis, financial KPI development, departmental reporting, dashboard design, and business analysis.
